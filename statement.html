Artist statement

The point of art is to have better dreams.

Statement

I make music videos, and now short films, about the questions that keep me up at night: what a mind is, how large the universe really is, and what we keep or lose as we build things that think. Each piece starts small, as a lyric I can't shake or a voice memo recorded on the side of the road, and grows into a song and then a film.

I treat generative tools the way earlier filmmakers treated cameras and cutting tables: as instruments, not authors. I write and rewrite the lyrics, design the characters and the look, plan every shot, then render, reject and reorder until the cut feels true. Left to themselves, these tools miss the parts that matter. The direction is the work.

Dreams are where we rehearse the future. If art is a dream we share, then an artist's job is to dream better ones: stranger, kinder, more honest about what is coming, and more hopeful about what we can still choose. That is what I'm reaching for, one song and one film at a time.

Adam Eivy

Bio

Adam Eivy is a Seattle-based artist and technologist. After two decades building software, along with comics, a card game and conference talks, he now writes and produces original songs and directs the music videos for them. He builds each film in PortOS, an open source creative system of his own design, and has written about the process on Substack. His first short films are in production.
